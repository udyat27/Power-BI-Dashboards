# ✈️ Airline Customer Satisfaction & Operations Dashboard

## 📖 The Project Story: Turning Turbulent Data into Actionable Insights

Imagine walking into an airline’s operations room and being handed a massive, chaotic CSV file. It’s filled with missing timestamps, inconsistent passenger classifications, fragmented customer survey scores, and raw flight metrics. The airline is facing a critical dilemma: **they know their customer satisfaction is slipping, but they don't know if it's due to operational delays, legroom, or gate logistics.**

As the data analyst on this project, my mission was to act as the air traffic controller for this data—taking it from a turbulent, messy state and guiding it to a smooth landing in a highly visual, actionable dashboard.

### The Flight Plan (My Process):
1. **Wrangling the Turbulence (Data Cleaning):** The raw dataset was rife with inconsistencies. I addressed missing values in arrival delays, standardized categorical names (such as fixing typos and alignment in travel types), and calculated overall satisfaction vs. dissatisfaction metrics.
2. **Finding the Altitude (Data Modeling):** I structured the data to easily segment passengers by their class (Business, Economy, Economy Plus) and their purpose of travel (Business vs. Personal) to see exactly who was experiencing the most friction.
3. **Designing the Cockpit (Dashboard Creation):** Using a cohesive aviation-blue palette, I designed a layout that prioritizes a user's cognitive load. The top and left panels act as the "Main Instrument Cluster"—giving executives an instant snapshot of delays, passenger volumes, and satisfaction rates before they dive into deeper correlations.

The result is a dynamic dashboard that allows airline stakeholders to stop guessing and start making data-driven decisions to improve the passenger experience.

## 📊 Project Summary

### 🎯 Objective
To clean, analyze, and visualize messy airline operational and customer sentiment data, transforming it into a cohesive business intelligence dashboard that identifies operational bottlenecks and key drivers of customer dissatisfaction.

### 🔑 Key Insights Delivered
* **The Dissatisfaction Warning:** Customer dissatisfaction sits at **56.55%**, outweighing the satisfaction rate (**43.45%**). This highlights an urgent need for operational intervention.
* **Demographic Split:** Business travel dominates the airline's passenger base at **69%**, compared to **31%** for personal travel. Notably, the airline successfully captures **62K Business Class** passengers.
* **The "Service" Bottleneck:** While In-Flight Entertainment and Leg Room score relatively well (~3.4 to 3.6 out of 5), **In-Flight Wi-Fi Service** lags significantly at **2.7/5**, marking it as a primary pain point for passengers.
* **Operational Latency:** Flights are averaging roughly **15 minutes of delay** for both departures and arrivals, a metric that directly correlates with the dipping satisfaction scores.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Handled missing values, standardized data types, and engineered metrics (Satisfaction vs. Dissatisfaction rates).
* **BI Tooling:** Power BI ]
* **UI/UX Dashboard Design:** Implemented KPI card hierarchies, consistent color-theming, and scannable visual real estate.
